*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

Too Many Cows is a free game made by Joe Shanahan (3Djoe.co.uk). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

## Information I collect

I don't collect any personal information. The game has no ads, no accounts and no sign-in, and it doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal details.

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to me.

## Unity

Too Many Cows is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may automatically collect limited technical information, such as your device model, operating system version, app version, and anonymous gameplay and crash data. This information is used to understand how the game performs and to fix bugs. It does not identify you personally, and I don't use it for advertising.

Unity handles this information under its own privacy policy:

- [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)

## Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, I'll update this page and change the date at the top.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email me at joe@3djoe.co.uk.
